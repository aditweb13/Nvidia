# ShopEasy Store Policies

## 1. Returns and Refunds
- **Full Refund Window (0–30 days):** Items returned within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date are eligible for a 100% full refund to the original payment method.
- **Store Credit Window (31–60 days):** Items returned between 31 and 60 days from the delivery date are eligible for ShopEasy Store Credit only. No cash or card refunds will be issued.
- **Past 60 Days:** Items delivered more than 60 days ago are not eligible for return, exchange, or refund.
- **Condition:** Items must be unworn, undamaged, in original packaging with tags intact (except for defective/damaged arrivals).
- **Return Shipping:** Standard return shipping fee is $5.99, deducted from the refund amount, unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

## 2. Damaged or Defective Items
- **Report Window:** Customers must report damaged, broken, or defective items within 7 days of delivery.
- **Remedy:** Customers may choose either a free immediate replacement shipment or a 100% full refund without any return fee.
- **Proof:** Photo or video evidence of the damaged package/item is required.

## 3. Billing & Duplicate Charges
- **Duplicate Charges:** If a customer's payment method was charged multiple times for the same transaction, the duplicate charge will be refunded immediately (funds typically reflect within 3–5 business days).
- **Authorization Holds:** Pending authorizations are automatically released by the customer's bank within 2–5 business days.

## 4. Coupons and Promotional Discounts
- **Checkout Policy:** Coupons and promo codes must be applied during checkout prior to placing the order.
- **Retroactive Adjustments:** If a customer forgot to enter a valid coupon code and contacts support within 24 hours of placing the order, support may issue a one-time courtesy store credit for the promotional difference.
- **Stacking:** Only one promotional coupon code can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be stacked with clearance or flash-sale discounts.

## 5. Order Cancellation & Modification
- **Eligible Statuses:** Orders can only be cancelled or modified if they are currently in `Pending` or `Processing` status.
- **Ineligible Statuses:** Once an order moves to `Shipped`, `In Transit`, or `Delivered`, it cannot be cancelled or redirected. The customer must receive the order and follow standard return procedures.

## 6. Shipping & Delivery
- **Standard Shipping:** 3–5 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** 1–2 business days.
- **Lost in Transit:** If an order has had no tracking scan update for more than 7 consecutive days, ShopEasy will declare it lost and issue a free reshipment or full refund.
